Privacy notice

It Takes a Village collective · last updated 7 October 2026

Who we are

This site and our apps are run by the It Takes a Village collective. If you have any question about your information, email broadfords@gmail.com.

This page

This page doesn't ask you for anything, doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you. Two things happen when it loads:

  • Our web host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs (such as your IP address) to keep the site running and secure.
  • The page's font comes from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for it. Google receives your IP address for that request.

Our apps

Each app only keeps what it needs to work. Depending on the app, that might include:

  • Settings and preferences, stored on your own phone.
  • A profile, if the app has one and you choose to fill it in.
  • Ideas you suggest and votes you cast in "Your App, your way". We check new suggestions before they're shown to other people.

Each app explains anything specific to it. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

If you email us

We use your email only to reply and to act on your idea or request. We keep it for as long as we need to deal with it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete information we hold about you. Email us and we'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
